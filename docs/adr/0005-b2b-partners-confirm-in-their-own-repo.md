# b2b partners confirm in their own repo

A set lives in the repo of the DJ who published it, and its `playedWith` names whoever they played back to back with. Naming someone isn't their word that they were there, so a b2b only counts once the partner confirms it: the site asks them in their Inbox, and accepting writes a small record in their own repo pointing at the set. Until then the set shows the partner's name unlinked, and the b2b isn't on the partner's profile or credited to them anywhere, `at:author` included. The set stays the publisher's to edit. A partner who wants their own account of the night publishes their own set and links the two, and a pairing that keeps playing together becomes an act.

## Considered options

- Copying the whole set into the partner's repo when they accept: both profiles show it, but the copies drift as soon as either DJ edits theirs.
- Moving the set into a root-owned space both DJs write to, as acts do ([0003](0003-profiles-in-repos-performers-in-spaces.md)): both can edit, but every b2b becomes a small act with a public copy the root has to keep in sync, and one-off b2bs aren't acts.
- Trusting `playedWith` as written: anyone could credit anyone with a set they never played.
