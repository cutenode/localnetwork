# Venue blocks are silent

A venue's owners can block a profile, act or party from listing events there, claiming a relationship to it, or naming it in booking inquiries. A block is on the protocol, like a Bluesky block: a `computer.localnetwork.venue.block` record the root writes into its own public repo, naming the venue's space and who's blocked, so anyone can read it there. Silent means unannounced. The blocked side is never told, and an event it lists at the venue looks like any other event still waiting for the venue. Telling someone they're blocked invites them to push back on the venue, or to come back under another name, and a venue shouldn't have to explain itself to keep someone out.

## Consequences

- The site enforces the block on listings, relationships and booking inquiries. A blocked party's event never shows as confirmed at that venue, and its organisers get no inbox item about the block.
- On the site, only the venue's people with the block capability and staff see the block list. Staff see blocks in the staff console, so they can tell a silent block from a venue that never answers.
- A block survives a venue merge. Forgetting a venue's space doesn't take its blocks away.
