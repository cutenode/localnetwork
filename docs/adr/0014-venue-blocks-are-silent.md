# Venue blocks are silent

A venue's owners can block a profile, act or party from listing events there, claiming a relationship to it, or naming it in booking inquiries. The blocked side is never told. An event it lists at the venue looks like any other event still waiting for the venue, and only the venue's owners and staff can see the block. Telling someone they're blocked invites them to push back on the venue, or to come back under another name, and a venue shouldn't have to explain itself to keep someone out.

## Consequences

- A blocked party's event never shows as confirmed at that venue, and its organisers get no inbox item about the block.
- Staff see blocks in the staff console, so they can tell a silent block from a venue that never answers.
- A block survives a venue merge.
