# Local Network

Tools for DJs and the people who book them. This glossary covers the website at localnetwork.computer.

## Site areas

**Public page**:
A page anyone can read without signing in. Viewers allowed to read more, like members or people granted a press kit, see more on it, but nobody sees controls for changing it there, apart from following or saving.
_Avoid_: profile view, read-only page

**Dashboard**:
The signed-in area where every change to what public pages show happens. There's one per account, and its sections follow what that account takes part in, whether as a manager, a promoter, or both.
_Avoid_: app, account area, my page, DJ dashboard, promoter dashboard

**Staff console**:
The separate area where staff moderate and run the site. Only staff can see it.
_Avoid_: admin dashboard, admin panel

## People and acts

**Project**:
A DJ act listed on the site: a solo DJ, a b2b duo, a group, or a collective.
_Avoid_: act, artist, DJ (in code and docs; "Find DJs" stays as marketing copy)

**Manager**:
An account that runs a project's listing, press kits, shows and booking threads. A project can have several, and adding or removing one changes what they can see everywhere.
_Avoid_: owner, member, admin

**Request**:
A listing, a claim, or a change to a project, filed for staff to review. A project's listing changes only through an approved request or a staff edit.
_Avoid_: proposal, submission, ticket

**Press kit**:
A project's pitch to promoters, with a public part anyone can read and a private part only granted viewers can. A project can have several, and all its managers share control of each one.
_Avoid_: EPK (in copy), media kit

**Backstage**:
A project's private area that only its current managers can read, where they keep work in progress such as drafts.
_Avoid_: workspace, managers' space, team space

**Draft**:
One manager's unpublished changes to a press kit, kept in backstage and visible to the project's other managers. Publishing applies only what it changed onto the published press kit.
_Avoid_: edit, revision, request (requests go to staff)

**Grant**:
Permission for one account to read one press kit's private part. Any of the project's managers can give or take it away.
_Avoid_: access, approval

**DJ**:
A person or account that plays, with the profile and sets they publish from locallibrary.
_Avoid_: project (when meaning the person)

**Artist**:
A producer credited in DJs' tracklists. Artists are not DJs or projects.
_Avoid_: DJ, act
