# localtoolbox

The Chrome extension DJs keep open while they work through SoundCloud: it shows what they already have, gathers what they want, and gets free downloads.

## Tracks

**Session queue**:
The tracks picked out while working through a page, held together until something is done with the whole batch. It ends with the browser.
_Avoid_: basket, cart, list (the Manager's list is another thing), playlist

**Mark**:
A record that a track was downloaded, which is what puts the marker on the page.
_Avoid_: flag, tick, downloaded state

**Details**:
What SoundCloud said about a track, kept for a while so it doesn't have to be asked again.
_Avoid_: metadata, track cache

## Downloads

**Gate**:
A page that puts a free download behind steps, such as following an artist or leaving an email address.
_Avoid_: wall, funnel, download page

**Walk**:
Taking one gate as far as it goes without a person.
_Avoid_: crawl, scrape, bypass

**Page gate**:
A gate the person does on its own page, with the extension outlining the steps, filling in the email and saving the file the page hands over. Droploud, Pump Your Sound and Laylo are done this way.
_Avoid_: assisted gate, manual gate

**Drop**:
A Laylo notify-me page: people leave an address and the artist sends the link out later. It isn't a gate; the person signs up on its page.
_Avoid_: release, campaign
