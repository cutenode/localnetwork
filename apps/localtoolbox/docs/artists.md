# Artists

**artists** keeps a list of the people behind your tracks, with the links they put on SoundCloud:
Instagram, Bandcamp, a website, and any web addresses in their bio. It's for following them,
asking for a track, or crediting them.

An artist here is a track's uploader. On a playlist full of reposts and bootlegs, that's the
channel, not whoever made the track.

## Adding artists

Nothing goes on the list by itself. The bar at the top offers three sources, each with a count:

- the SoundCloud playlist, Likes page or track page in front (**refresh** once its artists are all
  on the list),
- **Manager**, the artists behind the Manager's list,
- **queue**, the artists behind the session queue.

Artists show up at once, then fill in from what the SoundCloud tab in front has loaded. A track
only names its uploader in short, so a row says **not loaded yet** until a SoundCloud page has
shown that artist, and **links not loaded yet** until you've opened their profile. Open their
SoundCloud page, then come back: the list fills in whenever the panel opens on a SoundCloud tab.

## The list

Each row has the artist's picture, name, where they are, how many follow them, and their links as
lettered badges. A badge opens that page.

The chevron opens a row into every link as an address, the bio, the place, the follower count and
the tracks that brought the artist in. Click any of them to copy it. Below are the same search
badges the Manager uses and **off the list**.

Search by name, place, bio, link or track title. Sort **by name**, **list order** or **most
followed**. **copy** in the header puts every artist the search shows on the clipboard as text:
the name, the profile and each link on its own line.

The list keeps up to two thousand artists, and goes in the file when you
[save everything](saving.md). Their profiles don't: they last until you close the browser, and the
next SoundCloud tab fills them in again.
