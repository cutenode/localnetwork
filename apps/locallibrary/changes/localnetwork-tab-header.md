### Changed

- The player's localnetwork view lists only the sections that can be published: Sets, Energy,
  Genres, Artists, Era and Collection. Tempo, Keys, Mixing, Set shape, Labels, Signature tracks,
  Prep, Player style and Summary aren't shown there any more (`locallibrary profile` still reports
  them).
- Its header is titled Profile and sums up the collection: tracks, genres, artists, the years
  they came out, and how many sets. The generated headline ("Dubstep and Riddim · 100–160 BPM"),
  the traits, the sources read and the document count are gone.
- The account bar says when to sign in again only in the last two days, as a warning.

### Removed

- `a` (profile from every drive or just the open one) and `m` (pin a signature track) in the
  localnetwork view. `h` now hides the selected artist under Artists, as `H` did.
