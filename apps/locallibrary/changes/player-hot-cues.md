### Added

- **Hot cues set from the pads.** In the player, an empty pad (`1`-`8` in player mode, or with
  `'`) sets its hot cue where the playhead is, on the nearest beat with quantize on, as on a CDJ.
  It shows at once and is written to the drive in the background, and `u` takes it back. In
  player mode, `del` followed by a pad deletes that hot cue (the cues turn red until a pad is
  pressed). `:cue` and `:del cue` do the same from any view.
