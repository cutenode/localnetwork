### Added

- **Hot cues set from the pads.** In the player, an empty pad (`1`-`8` in player mode, or with
  the pads on) sets its hot cue where the playhead is, on the nearest beat with quantize on, as on
  a CDJ. It shows at once and is written to the drive in the background, and `u` takes it back.
  In player mode, `del` and then a pad deletes that hot cue (the hot cues turn red until a pad is
  pressed; `del` again or esc deletes none). "set a hot cue here" and "delete a hot cue" in `:`
  do the same from any view, and both can be given keys of their own.
