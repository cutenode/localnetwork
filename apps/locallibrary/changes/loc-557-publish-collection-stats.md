### Changed

- **Your published profile describes your collection, not your plays.** Publish now sends only
  Energy, Genres, Artists, Era and Sets, plus Collection if you turn it on. Genres, Artists, Era and
  Energy count each track in your collection once, played or not, so a track you played ten times
  no longer counts ten times, and one played off a drive locallibrary hasn't seen doesn't skew
  them. Energy is the mix of high, mid and low mood across every collection track with rekordbox's
  phrase analysis. Summary, Tempo, Set shape, Mixing, Keys, Labels, Signature tracks, Prep and
  Player style are no longer published, and `profile include` refuses them. They still show in
  `locallibrary profile` and the player. Artists and Collection stay off until you include them.
  If your saved choices list a section that can't be published any more, it's ignored. The profile
  document is now version 2.
