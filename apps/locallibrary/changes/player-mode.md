### Changed

- **Player mode.** The Deck layout is called player mode, and `f` opens it from any tab: closing it
  (`f` or `esc`) goes back to the tab it was opened from. The status line says `PLAYER`.
- **Less on screen in player mode.** The file's format, size and drive, its genre, rating, play
  count, date added, comment and credits are left to the info panel (`i`), and the analysis panel
  is gone: the waveforms show what it counted. The readout under the waveforms drops the elapsed
  time (the time left is large beside the tempo), the waveform's colours (`w` says them as they
  change), the volume at 100%, and every mode that's off. The footer keeps to hot cues, loop,
  pause, play next, browse, back and the keys.
- **The beat ruler sits on the waveform.** The cue markers go above it, so its ticks, bar numbers
  and the playhead's marker run straight into the waveform, and each bar's line carries on down
  through the waveform where it's empty.

### Added

- **Footer hints are buttons.** A click on a hint's key or its label is that key; a range like
  `1-8` isn't one. A click on the `LOOP` chip under the waveforms ends the loop.
