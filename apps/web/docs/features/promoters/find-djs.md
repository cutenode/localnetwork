# Find DJs

Directory search (Find DJs, `/djs`) over active projects and the DJs staff list, together, by text, genre family (a search for "house" finds deep house and afro house), distance from a venue, fee tier, slot, format, and type. A listed DJ counts as a solo DJ, found by the genres their profile names or else the ones they play most, and a solo project they manage alone gives way to their profile.
