# Spaces for acts, parties and venues

Acts, parties and venues each get a space under the root. Their pages ship dark behind two release flags: `spacePages` for the public act, party, venue and event pages, and `peopleSpaces` for the member pages (inviting people, invite links, asking to join, people lists). A route behind one calls `requireFlag` (`src/lib/server/http.ts`), which answers 404 while the flag is off for the viewer. A performer's space also holds the root's `computer.localnetwork.performer` record, which says whether it's an act (with what its page shows) or a DJ's own space for their backstage and managers. A venue's space holds the root's `computer.localnetwork.venue` record: its name, address and coordinates, its Foursquare place once known, and whether events naming it show at once (Anyone can list) or only once confirmed (Venue approves). Names repeat ([ADR 0016](../../../../../docs/adr/0016-names-repeat-keys-and-handles-identify.md)): an act, party or venue goes by its space key, and its page is `/<kind>/<key>/<name>` (`spacePagePath` in `src/lib/space-paths.ts`), where the name part is only there to read: any other name, or none, redirects to the current one. Anyone signed in can add a venue, which puts nobody in its space (owning one takes a confirmed claim). Adding a place that's already a venue gives back that venue, and so does adding the same name in the same city and country unless the two are different Foursquare places. Forms pick one with the venue picker (`src/lib/components/VenuePicker.svelte`): an existing venue by name, with its city on a second line to tell same-named ones apart, or a new one by its name and its city from the city search, both through `/api/venues`, which answers 404 while `spacePages` is off. A new venue's Foursquare place stays empty until staff add it. A party's space holds the root's `computer.localnetwork.party` record: its basics (name, picture, bio, area and links) and, once it's published, when. Without that it's a draft, which only its people and staff can open; everyone else gets a 404. Its page, `/party/<key>/<name>`, is the first built in place ([docs/edit-mode.md](../../edit-mode.md)): its people with page.edit, or staff with Edit as staff, edit its name, picture, bio, area and links where they're shown, and Publish a draft. It also shows its hosts and cohosts who haven't hidden. `/party/new` (signed in) opens the same page empty in edit mode: Save draft starts the party as a draft with its creator as host and goes on editing it at its own address, and Publish starts it published. The dashboard's sidebar offers it while `spacePages` is on. A party's events have their pages under it at `/party/<key>/<event>/<name>`, the event's record key and then its name to read, built in place the same way (`src/lib/server/event-page.ts`): its people with events.manage edit the name, the venue, the date and times (on the venue's clock, or until there's a venue, a time zone picked with them, which a new draft takes from its party's area, else its creator's, else UTC), the lineup (each performer by handle or act page link), the ticket link, what it's about and who can see it, and outside edit mode copy or reset a private event's link and cancel a published one. Its people and staff open any of its events; anyone else opens a published public one, or a private one from its link's `?t=` token, and gets a 404 for the rest. The page says whether the venue has confirmed it or it's waiting for the venue, and never more (ADR 0014), and links the venue's page. A listed event (published, public, not Never confirmed, and under Venue approves only once confirmed) has a public copy, a `community.lexicon.calendar.event` the root keeps in its own repo under the event's record key with its name, what it's about, its times, its status, the venue's address, coordinates and page, and its ticket link and page here (`calendarCopy` in `src/lib/server/services/events.ts`), so other calendar apps can show it. Each save and the venue confirming it rewrite it, and it's deleted once the event isn't listed. What changes listing without either (the night ending while it waits for the venue, the venue's mode or blocks) is caught by `reconcileCalendarCopies`, which runs at startup, every hour, and for the venue after its settings change, and writes only where a copy differs. While it's listed, until its night is over, and unless it's cancelled, the page has Going and Interested beside its tickets, with how many said each (`src/lib/server/services/rsvps.ts`): each writes a `community.lexicon.calendar.rsvp`, the record other AT Protocol calendar apps write, to the guest's own repo, answering the public copy, and pressing the pressed one takes the answer back. They're forms, so they work without scripts; signed out, they link to sign-in and back. A private event has neither, since an RSVP in a guest's public repo would show anyone who's going. An RSVP to a copy that's since been taken down stays in the guest's repo, counted nowhere. A night ends at 6:00 the next morning on the venue's clock (`nightEnd` in `src/lib/server/venue-time.ts`), and an event still waiting for the venue then is Never confirmed: it leaves every list, and only its party's people and staff can open it, marked so. A confirmed one just becomes a past event. The party's page lists its events until their night ends, its drafts, private ones and ones never confirmed marked for its people, and `/party/<key>/new` opens an empty event page in edit mode for its people with events.manage: Save draft makes the event as a draft and Publish makes it published. A save that would move the times onto another clock (picking a venue in another time zone, another venue, or another zone for the draft) comes back asking whether to keep them as entered or at the same moments, and each answer saves again. A venue's page, `/venue/<key>/<name>`, shows its address, an OpenStreetMap link when its coordinates are known, and the events listed there until their night ends, each with its party and marked waiting for the venue or Confirmed. It links the venue's dashboard pages for the people who can use them (claiming it for anyone else signed in) while `peopleSpaces` is on, and leaves its owners off until they can choose to show. A performer's booking details go in a separate booking details space with the same key, which only the root is in, so members can't read them from another client ([ADR 0011](../../../../../docs/adr/0011-booking-details-stay-out-of-the-performer-space.md)). Each space's people records say who's in it, in what role, with which capabilities, and whether they're hidden from everyone outside it. The root writes every record there, for someone who holds the capability the change needs or for staff, whose changes the audit log marks with their role. Someone always has to be left who can manage its people, unless staff decide otherwise. The space's members follow its people: everyone reads, and those with a capability that writes also write. The app repeats that at startup and every hour, so a member update that failed part way still finishes. The `pauseReconcilers` flag stops that, and every other round that writes to the root's repo to fix drift, without a deploy. The venue blocks round keeps running, since it only reads the root's blocks into the index.
