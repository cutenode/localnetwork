# Listings

When staff list one, the root writes a `computer.localnetwork.listing` record to its public repo with what its public page shows, the people in a listed role (an act's members, a party's hosts and cohosts) who haven't hidden, and an act's managers who haven't. It's rewritten only when that changes, and checked every hour. A listing points at a public page, so none is written until `spacePages` is on for signed-out visitors, and turning it off takes them down at the next hourly check. Acts, parties and venues have something to list: an act's form, basics and performer sections, a published party's basics (each avatar is a blob the root uploaded), and a venue's name, address and coordinates. A published party is public at its address before it's listed; a draft is never listed. A venue is listed as soon as it's added, since it's a public place, and a DJ's own space is never listed. HappyView keeps only the root's listings (`lua/computer/localnetwork/listing.lua`). Staff see every space and its people, hidden ones marked, at `/admin/spaces`, where they add and remove people, change roles, and list or unlist it.
