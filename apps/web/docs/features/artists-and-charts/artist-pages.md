# Artist pages

`/artists/<mbid>` (or `/artists/name/<name>` for an artist sets give no MusicBrainz ID) shows who played an artist's tracks: each of their tracks that appears in a published set, with every play (the DJ's profile and the projects they manage, the date, the venue and city linking the set's page, the kind of set, and how far into the set it starts, where the set knows), and their all-time supports. `/artists` finds them by name. A collaboration (`A & B`, `A x B`, `A vs. B`, `A and B`) counts for each of its acts when every one of them is credited alone in some set and MusicBrainz doesn't call the whole credit one artist, so "Ninajirachi & Porter Robinson" counts for both and "Chase & Status" stays one act.
