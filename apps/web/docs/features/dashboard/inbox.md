# Inbox

The inbox (`/dashboard/inbox`) tells each participant in a booking thread about a new inquiry, message or status change from someone else in it, with a dot in the sidebar while anything is unread. It shows one row per thread, saying in a sentence what happened since you last read it. An item names the performer, never which of its managers wrote, and holds none of what was written. Opening a row, or its thread, marks the thread read; it doesn't change whose turn it is. Read items go after 90 days and unread ones after 180. People can also have new items emailed: the address comes from their account's host, read only after they approve it, and nothing goes to it until they open a link we email it, which works for a day. New items wait two minutes so a burst goes as one email. Anything older than six hours when it's indexed stays on the site. They can also have them sent as Bluesky DMs from the root account, a minute apart at most; when Bluesky chat says the root can't DM them yet, the settings page asks them to allow DMs from people they follow and to follow the root. The site asks chat alone, not the AppView, so it doesn't know which of the two is missing. `/dashboard/account/notifications` has a grid of inbox sections by Email and Bluesky DM that saves as you tick, and turns each of the two on and off and sends a test. Email and DMs roll out an account at a time behind the `inboxEmail` and `inboxDm` flags. Where a channel's flag is off for someone, the settings page doesn't offer it (only turning it off, if they turned it on before), and the outbox skips what would have gone to them by it: it stays in their site inbox, and turning the flag back on sends only what comes after.
