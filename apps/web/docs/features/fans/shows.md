# Shows near you

`/shows` lists upcoming shows by date near a city (picked with the same city search as the directory, and remembered in a cookie the home page reads too), or, under Following, the shows of the projects you follow, nearest first once you pick a city. A show drops off once it ends, or 12 hours after it starts when it has no end. While `spacePages` is on, parties' published public events are merged in by date as soon as they're published (a party doesn't need staff to list it for this), each linking its page, party and venue and marked waiting for the venue or Confirmed, until its night ends (`src/lib/server/events-list.ts`). A page holds up to 30, and ends early where the directory's page of shows does, since its next page could start before the next event. Following lists only shows. There are no email or push notifications for shows yet.
