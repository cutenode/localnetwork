# Moderation

Moderators review creates, claims, and changes with field-level diffs and stale-revision warnings. Someone who runs a venue claims it at `/dashboard/venues/<key>/claim` (behind `peopleSpaces`) with the same evidence a project claim takes, and approving it adds them to the venue's space as an owner. At `/dashboard/venues/<key>/settings` owners choose Anyone can list (events naming the venue show at once, waiting for the venue until confirmed) or Venue approves (they show only once confirmed), and block people, acts and parties. A block is a public `computer.localnetwork.venue.block` record in the root's repo, like a Bluesky block, indexed from there at startup and hourly. `VenueRules` (`src/lib/server/services/venue-rules.ts`) is what event, relationship and inquiry code asks, and a blocked side's result looks like any event still waiting for the venue (ADR 0014). A party's events are root-written `computer.localnetwork.event` records in its space, made, edited, published and cancelled by its people with events.manage or staff (`EventService`). An event starts as a draft, which only the party's people and staff see, and publishing one needs a name, a date, a venue and a published party. A draft without a venue keeps its times on its own time zone (its party's area's, else its creator's, else UTC), and the venue's clock takes over once there is one; a save that would move the times to other moments has to say whether to keep the clock times or the moments. A private event is never listed and opens for anyone with its link, whose token is compared in constant time (ADR 0015); resetting the link gives it a new token, so old links stop working. Approvers see the upcoming events waiting for the venue at `/dashboard/venues/<key>/events`, and an approval is a record in the venue's own space. Nobody can review their own request.
