locallibrary

A desktop and command-line tool for DJs that keeps rekordbox USB drives in sync, backed up with full history, and merged, and works out what your sets say about how you play.

Signing in

This address is how locallibrary introduces itself when you sign in with your Atmosphere account, the account you use for Bluesky and other AT Protocol apps. You sign in on your own account's server, in your browser: locallibrary never sees your password.

locallibrary only asks to know who you are. It can't post or change anything on your account, and you can sign out with locallibrary account logout or remove it from your account's settings at any time.

The details servers read are in oauth-client-metadata.json.
